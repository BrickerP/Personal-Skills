# Referral message templates

Each variant is <=80 words. Replace bracketed fields and dry-run before sending.

## Variant 1 — direct role fit

Hi [Name] — I saw [Company] is hiring for [Role]. I’m a US permanent resident targeting Bay Area AI infrastructure roles, with production experience across agent tooling, backend APIs, auth/billing, and reliability. If you think my background fits, would you be open to referring me or pointing me to the right team? Resume/JD-specific summary available. Thanks!

## Variant 2 — engineering signal

Hi [Name], I’m interested in [Company]’s [Role]. My recent work is in production AI platform/backend systems: tool-use workflows, API contracts, OAuth/billing, evaluation gates, and cloud reliability. I’m a US permanent resident and can work in the Bay Area/US without sponsorship. Would you be open to a referral or quick routing advice?

## Variant 3 — shared domain

Hi [Name] — your work around [domain] caught my eye. I’m applying to [Company]’s [Role] and have built production agent-infrastructure and backend platform systems end-to-end. If my background seems relevant, could I send a concise resume summary for a possible referral? I’m a US permanent resident and prefer email follow-up.

## Variant 4 — concise ask

Hi [Name], I’m exploring [Company]’s [Role]. I’m a Bay Area-targeted AI infra/backend engineer with production experience in agent tooling, API contracts, auth/billing, evaluation, and reliability. Would you be comfortable referring me, or should I apply directly and mention your team’s work as context? Happy to send a 5-line fit summary.

## Variant 5 — founder/ownership angle

Hi [Name], I’m interested in [Role] at [Company]. I’ve owned production AI/backend systems from API contracts and auth/billing through deployment and QA, plus founder-level full-stack product work. I’m a US permanent resident targeting Bay Area/Remote US roles. If there’s a fit, would you consider a referral or suggest the best application path?
