## Limits

- Topic and pattern labels are rule-derived, not perfect semantic classifications.
- `summary_auto` fields are derived signals, not article summaries.
- Titles, categories, tags, URLs, dates, and counts are source-backed metadata; topic labels and reasoning patterns are inference.
- For exact claims about a particular article, fetch the public URL and summarize within copyright limits.
- The framework may contain ideological bias from the source corpus. Use it as a lens, not as an objective truth engine.

## Regeneration

```bash
python3 scripts/ingest_public_free_articles.py
python3 scripts/audit_skill.py
```
